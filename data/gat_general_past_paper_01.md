## Section 1: Verbal Reasoning (English)

### Part 1: Analogies (Question 1 to Question 5)
Directions: Select the pair that best expresses a relationship similar to that expressed in the original pair.

#### Question 1
**CANDID : DECEPTION ::**
- A) arrogant : pride
- B) frugal : waste
- C) timid : hesitation
- D) flexible : change

**Correct Key:** B
**Domain:** Verbal Reasoning
**Skill:** Analogies (Antonymic Relation)
**Explanation:** Someone who is candid (honest, frank) is devoid of deception. Similarly, someone who is frugal (economical) avoids and is devoid of waste.

#### Question 2
**ARCHIPELAGO : ISLANDS ::**
- A) galaxy : stars
- B) desert : dunes
- C) forest : leaves
- D) continent : countries

**Correct Key:** A
**Domain:** Verbal Reasoning
**Skill:** Analogies (Part-to-Whole / Collection)
**Explanation:** An archipelago is a geographical formation specifically composed of a cluster of islands. Similarly, a galaxy is composed of billions of stars.

#### Question 3
**INCEPTION : CONCLUSION ::**
- A) departure : arrival
- B) preamble : clause
- C) prologue : narrative
- D) culmination : peak

**Correct Key:** A
**Domain:** Verbal Reasoning
**Skill:** Analogies (Antonymic Sequence)
**Explanation:** Inception marks the beginning of an event; conclusion marks its formal end. Similarly, departure marks the start of a journey and arrival marks its end.

#### Question 4
**SURGEON : SCALPEL ::**
- A) carpenter : wood
- B) sculptor : chisel
- C) author : book
- D) pilot : airport

**Correct Key:** B
**Domain:** Verbal Reasoning
**Skill:** Analogies (Agent-to-Tool)
**Explanation:** A surgeon uses a scalpel as a primary precision instrument to cut and reshape tissue. Likewise, a sculptor uses a chisel to cut and shape stone or wood.

#### Question 5
**TACITURN : SPEECH ::**
- A) loquacious : talk
- B) sedentary : movement
- C) pious : belief
- D) voracious : appetite

**Correct Key:** B
**Domain:** Verbal Reasoning
**Skill:** Analogies (Negative Attribute)
**Explanation:** A person who is taciturn avoids or produces little speech. Similarly, someone who is sedentary avoids or engages in minimal physical movement.

### Part 2: Sentence Completion (Question 6 to Question 8)

#### Question 6
Although the professor lecture was undeniably _______, several students struggled to remain awake due to its sheer _______.
- A) redundant ... brevity
- B) illuminating ... duration
- C) superficial ... depth
- D) tedious ... excitement

**Correct Key:** B
**Domain:** Verbal Reasoning
**Skill:** Sentence Completion (Contrast Signal)
**Explanation:** The word Although signals a contrast. If students struggled to stay awake despite the lecture being high quality (illuminating), the cause was its excessive length (duration).

#### Question 7
The CEO refused to accept the preliminary audit report, criticizing it as _______ because it omitted key financial liabilities incurred during the fourth quarter.
- A) comprehensive
- B) partisan
- C) cursory
- D) definitive

**Correct Key:** C
**Domain:** Verbal Reasoning
**Skill:** Sentence Completion (Cause and Effect)
**Explanation:** The report omitted essential liabilities, so the CEO rejected it as superficial or hasty (cursory).

#### Question 8
Known for his _______ temperament, the magistrate remained unflappable even amid vehement protests from defense counsel.
- A) volatile
- B) placid
- C) contentious
- D) capricious

**Correct Key:** B
**Domain:** Verbal Reasoning
**Skill:** Sentence Completion (Definition Clue)
**Explanation:** Unflappable amid protests indicates calmness. Placid means serenely calm and unexcitable.

### Part 3: Reading Comprehension (Question 9 to Question 10)

Passage:
During the early Industrial Revolution in 19th-century Britain, urbanization proceeded at an unprecedented pace, outpacing the municipal capacity to provide sanitary infrastructure. Dense worker tenements lacked piped potable water, underground sewage networks, and ventilation. Medical authorities initially subscribed to the miasma theory, which posited that epidemic diseases such as cholera and typhus were propagated by noxious airborne vapors emanated from decaying organic matter. It was not until the landmark 1854 epidemiological investigation of the Broad Street pump by Dr. John Snow that the waterborne etiology of cholera was empirically established. Snow mapped cholera clusters and traced the outbreak to a single municipal pump contaminated by an adjacent cesspool. This empirical triumph not only catalyzed the sanitary reform movement but also laid the foundation of modern quantitative epidemiology.

- A) microbial agents in communal water supplies

**Correct Key:** B
**Domain:** Verbal Reasoning
**Skill:** Reading Comprehension (Explicit Fact Retrieval)
**Explanation:** The passage states that the miasma theory posited that epidemic diseases were propagated by noxious airborne vapors from decaying organic matter.

#### Question 10
The author references Dr. John Snow 1854 investigation primarily in order to:
- A) critique the slow pace of parliamentary sanitary legislation in Britain
- B) illustrate how empirical spatial mapping disproved the prevailing theory of cholera transmission

## Section 2: Quantitative Reasoning (Mathematics)

### Part 1: Arithmetic, Ratios and Percentages

#### Question 11
A water tank is fitted with two inlet pipes, Pipe X and Pipe Y. Pipe X can fill the empty tank in 12 hours, while Pipe Y can fill it in 18 hours. If both pipes are opened simultaneously when the tank is empty, how many hours will it take to fill the tank completely?
- A) 6.8 hours
- B) 7.2 hours
- C) 7.5 hours
- D) 8.0 hours

**Correct Key:** B
**Domain:** Quantitative Reasoning
**Skill:** Work and Rate Problems
**Formula:** 1 / T_net = 1/12 + 1/18 = (3 + 2)/36 = 5/36.
**Calculation:** T_net = 36 / 5 = 7.2 hours.

#### Question 12
The average (arithmetic mean) test score of a class of 30 students is 76. If the highest score of 98 and the lowest score of 40 are excluded, what is the new average score of the remaining 28 students?
- A) 75.0
- B) 76.5
- C) 77.0
- D) 78.0

**Correct Key:** B
**Domain:** Quantitative Reasoning
**Skill:** Arithmetic Mean / Statistics
**Calculation:**
- Total sum of 30 students = 30 * 76 = 2280.
- Sum of excluded scores = 98 + 40 = 138.
- Remaining sum for 28 students = 2280 - 138 = 2142.
- New mean = 2142 / 28 = 76.5.

#### Question 13
If the price of petrol increases by 25 percent, by what percentage must a motorist reduce petrol consumption so that the total monthly expenditure on petrol remains unchanged?
- A) 20 percent
- B) 25 percent
- C) 16.67 percent
- D) 30 percent

**Correct Key:** A
**Domain:** Quantitative Reasoning
**Skill:** Percentage Expenditure Invariance
**Formula:** Reduction % = [r / (100 + r)] * 100% = [25 / 125] * 100% = (1/5) * 100% = 20%.

#### Question 14
Two trains, each 150 meters long, are traveling on parallel tracks in opposite directions with uniform speeds of 54 km/h and 36 km/h respectively. How many seconds will they take to completely pass each other from the moment their engines meet?
- A) 10 seconds
- B) 12 seconds
- C) 15 seconds
- D) 18 seconds

**Correct Key:** B
**Domain:** Quantitative Reasoning
**Skill:** Relative Speed & Distance
**Calculation:**
- Relative speed = 54 + 36 = 90 km/h.
- In m/s: 90 * (5/18) = 25 m/s.
- Total distance = 150 + 150 = 300 meters.
- Time = Distance / Speed = 300 / 25 = 12 seconds.

### Part 2: Algebra and Geometry

#### Question 15
If 3^(2x - 1) = 27^(x - 2), what is the value of x?
- A) 3
- B) 5
- C) 7
- D) 9

**Correct Key:** B
**Domain:** Quantitative Reasoning
**Skill:** Exponents and Equating Bases
**Calculation:**
- 27^(x-2) = (3^3)^(x-2) = 3^(3x - 6).
- Equate powers: 2x - 1 = 3x - 6 => 3x - 2x = 6 - 1 => x = 5.

#### Question 16
In a right-angled triangle ABC, the length of the hypotenuse AC is 25 cm, and the length of base BC is 24 cm. A circle is inscribed inside triangle ABC. What is the radius of the inscribed circle?
- A) 2 cm
- B) 3 cm
- C) 4 cm
- D) 5 cm

**Correct Key:** B
**Domain:** Quantitative Reasoning
**Skill:** Geometry (Incircle of Right Triangle)
**Calculation:**
- Perpendicular side AB = sqrt(25^2 - 24^2) = sqrt(625 - 576) = sqrt(49) = 7 cm.
- For right triangle with legs a, b and hypotenuse c: Inradius r = (a + b - c) / 2 = (7 + 24 - 25) / 2 = 6 / 2 = 3 cm.

## Section 3: Analytical Reasoning

### Scenario 1: Medical Conference Presentations
Seven research fellows—Dr. Asad, Dr. Bilal, Dr. Chand, Dr. Danish, Dr. Ehsan, Dr. Faisal, and Dr. Ghazala—must present papers sequentially from 1st to 7th at an international symposium.
The presentation order is subject to the following institutional rules:
1. Dr. Faisal must present immediately before Dr. Bilal ([F][B]).
2. Dr. Danish cannot present in the 1st or 7th position.
3. Exactly two fellows must present between Dr. Asad and Dr. Chand.
4. Dr. Ghazala must present earlier than Dr. Danish (G < D).
5. Dr. Ehsan must present in the 4th position (E = 4).

#### Question 17
Which one of the following is an acceptable chronological order of presenters from 1st to 7th?
- A) Ghazala, Asad, Danish, Ehsan, Chand, Faisal, Bilal
- B) Asad, Faisal, Bilal, Ehsan, Ghazala, Danish, Chand
- C) Ghazala, Asad, Faisal, Bilal, Ehsan, Danish, Chand
- D) Faisal, Bilal, Ghazala, Danish, Asad, Ehsan, Chand

**Correct Key:** A
**Domain:** Analytical Reasoning
**Skill:** Rule Checking & Acceptable Ordering
**Explanation:**
Checking Option A (Ghazala, Asad, Danish, Ehsan, Chand, Faisal, Bilal):
1. Faisal immediately before Bilal: Yes (slots 6 and 7).
2. Danish not 1st or 7th: Yes (slot 3).
3. Exactly two between Asad and Chand: Asad is 2nd, Chand is 5th (slots 3 and 4 are between them, exactly 2).
4. Ghazala before Danish: Yes (slot 1 < slot 3).
5. Ehsan is 4th: Yes (slot 4).
All rules are satisfied.

#### Question 18
If Dr. Danish presents in the 6th position, who must present in the 7th position?
- A) Dr. Asad
- B) Dr. Bilal
- C) Dr. Chand
- D) Dr. Faisal

**Correct Key:** C
**Domain:** Analytical Reasoning
**Skill:** Hypothetical Deduction
**Explanation:** If Danish is 6th and Ehsan is 4th, the block [F][B] must be placed in slots (1, 2) or (2, 3). If [F][B] is in (2, 3), slots 1, 5, 7 remain. To have exactly two slots between Asad and Chand, Asad and Chand must be in slots 1 and 4 or 4 and 7, but 4 is Ehsan. Thus with slots 1, 5, 7, the only pair separated by 2 intervening positions is (1, 4) or (2, 5) or (3, 6) or (4, 7). From available slots {1, 5, 7}, Chand can only occupy slot 7 with Asad in slot 4 (impossible) or if [F][B] occupies (1, 2), then available are 3, 5, 7. Positions 3 and 6 have two between them, but 6 is Danish. Positions 5 and 7 cannot have 2 between them. Thus Chand must be in slot 7.

#### Question 19
If Dr. Asad presents 1st, in which position could Dr. Chand present?
- A) 3rd
- B) 4th
- C) 5th
- D) 6th

**Correct Key:** B
**Domain:** Analytical Reasoning
**Skill:** Slot Spacing Calculation
**Explanation:** If Dr. Asad is 1st, exactly two fellows must be between Asad and Chand (slots 2 and 3). Therefore, Dr. Chand must present in the 4th position. (Note that because slot 4 is already occupied by Dr. Ehsan, Dr. Asad cannot actually present 1st in any valid full schedule).

### Scenario 2: Executive Audit Committee
A technology startup needs to form an executive audit committee of 4 members from a pool of 7 candidates:
- 3 Software Engineers: Tariq, Usman, Waqas
- 4 Finance Specialists: Xara, Yasir, Zain, Rida

The selection is bound by the following conditions:
1. The committee must contain at least 1 Software Engineer and at least 2 Finance Specialists.
2. If Tariq is chosen, Xara cannot be chosen.
3. At least one of Usman or Yasir must be on the committee.
4. Zain is selected if and only if Waqas is selected.

#### Question 20
Which of the following constitutes a valid 4-member committee?
- A) Tariq, Xara, Yasir, Zain
- B) Tariq, Yasir, Zain, Rida
- C) Tariq, Usman, Yasir, Rida
- D) Tariq, Yasir, Rida, Waqas

**Correct Key:** C
**Domain:** Analytical Reasoning
**Skill:** Grouping & Conditional Selection
**Explanation:**
- Option A violates Rule 2 (Tariq and Xara cannot both be chosen).
- Option B violates Rule 4 (Zain is chosen without Waqas).
- Option D violates Rule 4 (Waqas is chosen without Zain).
- Option C has Tariq, Usman (2 Engineers) and Yasir, Rida (2 Finance). Satisfies Rule 1 (>=1 Eng, >=2 Fin), Rule 2 (Tariq without Xara), Rule 3 (Usman and Yasir both present), Rule 4 (neither Zain nor Waqas). Thus C is completely valid.
